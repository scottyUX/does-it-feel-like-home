Before concluding

What these results can and cannot say

  • One Chinese lab. Both Chinese models are Qwen (Alibaba), so “origin” may be Qwen’s house style. A second Chinese lab would separate the two.
  • Photos. The Qwen models often left placeholder boxes where photos go, which drives the image and color differences.
  • Automatic measures. They are first-pass instruments; a hand-coded sample would check them.
  • Arena’s template. Every Arena site starts from the same React template, and its prompts are mostly apps and games.
  • Next. Compare with real Chinese and US websites of the same types, then ask people.
